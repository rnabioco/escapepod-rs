- **`summary`/`inspect summary` over a directory, `classify`'s POD5 index, `filter`'s portability check and `bam-filter`'s full scan no longer do their I/O one file (or one BGZF block) at a time (#465).** `summary` and `inspect summary` open and scan inputs on the rayon pool and fold results in input order, so output is unchanged; both gain `-t/--threads`. `filter` used to open every input once just to ask `nonuniform_signal_batch()` and then walk each footer again; the answer now comes from the reader `filter_files` already holds (`FilterResult::nonuniform_inputs`), so the "not portable" warning is printed after the filter rather than before it. `Pod5Index::build` opens and looks up files in parallel and merges in path order, so `storage_key` ordering (#334) is unchanged. `bam-filter` full scans inflate BGZF on a worker pool sized from the rayon pool (it was one thread); `clippy.toml` now bans `noodles_bam::io::Reader::new` for the same reason. Measured: `summary` 0.82 s to 0.27 s warm, `bam-filter` scan 6.5 s to 2.05 s on a 695 MB BAM, `filter` warm 2.1 s of redundant footer walk removed; cold-filesystem effects are neutral within noise (see the PR).
