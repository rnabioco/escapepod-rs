- **Native CRF encoder is 1.3-1.5x faster per read (and `escpod demux` ~28% less wall and CPU on the 20k dual-axis set).** The LSTM input projections and the linear head were rank-1 per-timestep loops that re-streamed their whole weight matrix at every timestep, which profiled at 66% of the encoder against 11% for the recurrence. They now run through `escapepod_signal::lstm::affine_rows`, a time-blocked kernel (AVX2 and AVX-512, runtime-dispatched) that keeps the accumulation order over `k` and rounds mul and add separately, so the scores are bit-identical to before on every backend, and so is the charging BiLSTM that shares `lstm.rs`. `CrfEncoder::encode_group` also scores straight into the caller's buffers instead of staging ~12 MB per group.
