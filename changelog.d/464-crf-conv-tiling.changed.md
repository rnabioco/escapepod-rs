- **Native CRF encoder convolutions are register-tiled (~17% faster per 8-read group, `escpod demux` ~9% less wall and CPU on the 20k dual-axis set).** The strided stage-3 convolution was an axpy per `(oc, ic, kk)` that loaded and stored its output row every time, which left it at 26% of the encoder, bound by loads and stores. It now keeps a 4-channel by 3-vector (AVX-512) or 4 by 2 (AVX2) tile of outputs in registers across the whole reduction. Accumulation order and separate mul/add rounding are unchanged, so the scores are bit-identical to before on every backend.
