- **`escpod demux --annotate` holds ~3x less memory per read (293 -> 89 B/read on a dual-axis `--ref-scores` run, ~11 GB less at 57 M reads).** The collected calls were five `HashMap<Uuid, _>` per axis, each repeating the 16-byte read id. They are now one shared id-to-slot map plus a `Vec` per column, handed to the sidecar writer through the new `ColumnValues::LabelCodesIndexed` / `ScoresIndexed` variants. Sidecars and the classifications CSV are byte-identical to before (rows sorted). At 20k reads the saving (~30 MB of 2.7 GB anonymous RSS) is below measurement noise; it is a scaling fix.
